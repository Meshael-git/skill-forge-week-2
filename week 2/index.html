SkillForge Semester 3 Programme

The SkillForge Semester 3 Programme is a structured training initiative designed to equip students with practical and industry-relevant digital skills.

This semester focuses on specialization, allowing participants to choose a program track and gain hands-on experience through real-world projects and guided mentorship.

Programme Highlights

  • Hands-on project-based learning
  • Industry-relevant skill development
  • Collaborative team environment
  • Portfolio building

About the Programme

SkillForge bridges the gap between theoretical education and real-world application. The programme is designed to help students develop practical skills that are highly demanded in today's digital economy.

Participants will engage in structured sessions, workshops, and group projects, all aimed at building competence and confidence.

Key Features

  • Interactive training sessions
  • Mentorship and guidance
  • Real-world project experience
  • Continuous assessment

Web Development Programme

This track focuses on building modern websites and web applications.

  • HTML, CSS, and responsive design
  • Introduction to JavaScript
  • Website structure and layout
  • Deployment basics

Graphics Design Programme

Develop creative skills in visual communication and digital design.

  • Design principles and color theory
  • Branding and logo design
  • Poster and digital graphics

Data Science Programme

Learn how to analyze, interpret, and visualize data effectively.

  • Data analysis fundamentals
  • Visualization techniques
  • Basic statistics

Cybersecurity Programme

Understand how to protect systems and digital information from threats.

  • Network security basics
  • Threat detection
  • Online safety practices

Eligibility Criteria

  • Registered student
  • Basic computer knowledge
  • Commitment to learning
